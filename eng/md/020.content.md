## Υ (id: ASL05446)

**Υ**, υ, ὖψιλόν, τό, indecl., 

*υpsilon, υ,*, the twentieth letter. As a numeral, υʹ \= 400, υ͵ \= 400,000\. At the beginning of a word, υ is always aspirated.

## υἱός (id: ASL05463)

**υἱός**, \-οῦ, ὁ, \[in LXX very freq. and nearly always for בֵּן, Ge 4:17, al.; for בַּר, Da LXX TH 7:13, al.; etc. ;], 

*a son*;

(b) υἱὸς τ. θεοῦ (cf. (as in Ps 8:5), means *a man*, one of the species, and indicates the human appearance of the person in question. It is used of the Messiah in Enoch, c. 46, §1–4, also in II Es 13:3, 12 , al. Our Lord first makes the phrase a title, using the def. art. It seems to combine the ideas of his true humanity and representative character. Exc. in Ac 7:56 and (anarth.) Re 1:13 14:14, it is used of Jesus only by himself: Mt 8:20, Mk 2:10, Lk 5:24, Jo 1:52, al.
