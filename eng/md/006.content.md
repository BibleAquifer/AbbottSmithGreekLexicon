## Ζ (id: ASL02347)

**Ζ**, ζ, ζῆτα, τό,

*zeta*, the sixth letter. As a numeral, ζ́ʹ \= ἑπτά, ἕβδομος (the obsol. *ϛ*, *Ϝ*, *vau*, "digamma," representing ἕξ), ζ́͵ \= 7000\.

*a belt*, *girdle*: Mt 3:4, Mk 1:6, Ac 21:11, Re 1:13 15:6; as a receptacle for money, Mt 10:9, Mk 6:8.†

* **Associated Passages:** Acts 21:11; Matthew 3:4; Matthew 10:9; Mark 1:6; Mark 6:8; Revelation 1:13; Revelation 15:6

**ζώννυμι and ζωννύω** \[in LXX chiefly for חגר;]

* **Associated Passages:** Acts 12:8; John 21:18


## ζωογονέω (id: ASL02382)

**ζωογονέω**, \-ῶ  (\< ζωός, *alive*, γένεσθαι), \[in LXX Ex 1:17, 18, 22, Jg 8:19, I Ki 2:6 27:9, 11, III Ki 21(20\):31, IV Ki 7:4 (חיה pi., hi.), Le 11:47 (חַיָּה)\*;] 

1\. in cl., *to engender*, *produce alive*, *endue with life*.

2\. In LXX and NT, *to preserve alive* (*DCG*, ii, 606a; Cremer, 274\): Lk 17:33, Ac 7:19, I Ti 6:13 (EV, *quickeneth*; R, mg., *preserveth*).†

* **Associated Passages:** 1 Kings 20:31; 1 Samuel 2:6; 1 Samuel 27:9; 1 Samuel 27:11; 1 Timothy 6:13; 2 Kings 7:4; Acts 7:19; Exodus 1:17; Exodus 1:18; Exodus 1:22; Judges 8:19; Leviticus 11:47; Luke 17:33


## ζῷον (id: ASL02383)

**ζῷον** (Rec. ζῶον, v. LS, s.v.), \-ου, τό  (\< ζωός, *alive*), \[in LXX for חַיָּה (chiefly) and cognate forms; freq. in Wi;] 

*a living creature*, *an animal*: Re 4:6–9 5:6 ff. Re 6:1 ff. Re 7:11 14:3 15:7 19:4; ζῴων τ. αἷμα, He 13:11; ἄλογα ζ., II Pe 2:12, Ju 10.†

*SYN.*: θηρίον, in which the brutal, bestial element is emphasized, and which is never used of sacrificial animals. On the other hand, ζ. is the more comprehensive, as expressing the vital element common to the whole animal creation (v. Tr., *Syn.*, lxxxi; Cremer, 274\).

* **Associated Passages:** 2 Peter 2:12; Hebrews 13:11; Jude 1:10; Revelation 4:6-9; Revelation 5:6; Revelation 6:1; Revelation 7:11; Revelation 14:3; Revelation 15:7; Revelation 19:4
