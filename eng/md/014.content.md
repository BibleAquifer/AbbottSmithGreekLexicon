## Ξ (id: ASL03722)

**Ξ**, ξ, ξῖ, τό, indecl.,

*Xi*, *x*, the fourteenth letter. As a numeral, ξʹ \= 60, ξ͵ \= 60,000\.



## ξενία (id: ASL03723)

\*\* **ξενία**, \-ας, ἡ  (\< ξένος), \[in LXX: Si 29:27 B1\*;] 

*hospitality*, *entertainment*: Phm 22 (cf. *ICC*, in l., but v. infr.). By meton., *a place of entertainment*, *a lodging\-place*: Ac 28:23, and so perh. Phm 22 (Lft., in l., *Phl.*, p. 9; but v. supr., and cf. MM, xvii).†

* **Associated Passages:** Acts 28:23; Philemon 1:22; Sirach 29:27


## ξενίζω (id: ASL03724)

\*\* **ξενίζω** (\< ξένος), \[in LXX: Es 3:13, Si 29:25, II Mac 9:6, III Mac 7:3\*;] 

1\. *to receive as a guest*, *entertain*: c. acc. pers., Ac 10:23 28:7, He 13:2; pass., Ac 10:6, 18, 32 21:16.

2\. In late writers (Polyb., al.; II Mac 9:6\), *to surprise*, *astonish* by strangeness: Ac 17:20; pass., I Pe 4:4, 12.†

* **Associated Passages:** 1 Peter 4:4; 1 Peter 4:12; 2 Maccabees 9:6; 3 Maccabees 7:3; Acts 10:6; Acts 10:18; Acts 10:23; Acts 10:32; Acts 17:20; Acts 21:16; Acts 28:7; Ezra 3:13; Hebrews 13:2; Sirach 29:25


## ξενοδοχέω (id: ASL03725)

\* **ξενοδοχέω** late Gk. for \-κέω  (\< ξένος, δέχομαι), 

*to entertain strangers*: I Ti 5:10.†

* **Associated Passages:** 1 Timothy 5:10


## ξένος (id: ASL03726)

**ξένος**, \-η, \-ον,  \[in LXX chiefly for נׇכְרִי;] 

(a) *foreign*, *alien*: δαιμόνια, Ac 17:18; διδαχαί, He 13:9;

(b) c. gen. rei, *strange to*, *estranged from*, *ignorant of* : Eph 2:12;

(c) *strange*, *unusual*: I Pe 4:12.

As subst., ὁ ξ., 

(a) *a foreigner*, *stranger*: Mt 25:35, 38, 43, 44 27:7, Ac 17:21, III Jo 5; ξένοι κ. τάροικοι (opp. to συμπολῖται, οίκεῖοι), Eph 2:19; ξ. καὶ παρεπίδημοι, He 11:13;

(b) one of the parties bound by ties of hospitality; 

α *the guest*;

β *the host* ( \= ξενοδόκος, Hom., *Il.*, xv, 532\): Ro 16:23.†

* **Associated Passages:** 1 Peter 4:12; 3 John 1:5; Acts 17:18; Acts 17:21; Ephesians 2:12; Ephesians 2:19; Hebrews 11:13; Hebrews 13:9; Matthew 25:35; Matthew 25:38; Matthew 25:43; Matthew 25:44; Matthew 27:7; Romans 16:23


## ξέστης (id: ASL03727)

\*\*† **ξέστης**, \-ου, ὁ  (a Sicilian corruption of Lat. sextarius), \[in Al.: Le 14:10 (לֹג, LXX κοτύλη)\*;] 

1\. *a sextarius* (about a pint).

2\. In NT, *a pitcher* of wood or stone: Mk 7:4.†

* **Associated Passages:** Leviticus 14:10; Mark 7:4


## ξηραίνω (id: ASL03728)

**ξηραίνω** (\< ξηρός), \[in LXX chiefly for יָבֵשׁ;] 

*to dry up*, *parch*, *wither*. c. acc., τ. χόρτον, Ja 1:11; pass., *to become* or *be dry* or *withered*: of plants, Mt 13:6 21:19, 20, Mk 4:6 11:20, 21, Lk 8:6, Jo 15:6, I Pe 1:24; of ripened crops, Re 14:15; of liquids, Mk 5:29, Re 16:12; of members of the body, *to waste away*, Mk 3:1 (cf. III Ki 13:4) 9:18.†

* **Associated Passages:** 1 Kings 13:4; 1 Peter 1:24; James 1:11; John 15:6; Luke 8:6; Matthew 13:6; Matthew 21:19; Matthew 21:20; Mark 3:1; Mark 4:6; Mark 5:29; Mark 9:18; Mark 11:20; Mark 11:21; Revelation 14:15; Revelation 16:12


## ξηρός (id: ASL03729)

**ξηρός**, \-ά, \-όν,  \[in LXX chiefly for יָבֵשׁ, its parts and derivatives, also for חָרָבָה, etc.;] 

*dry*: metaph. (of a sinner), ξύλον ξ., Lk 23:31; of members of the body shrunken by disease, *withered*: Jo 5:3; of the hand, Mt 12:10, Mk 3:3, Lk 6:6, 8; of the dry land, ἡ ξηρά (sc. γῆ, cf. Ge 1:9, 10; Jh 1:19, al.): Mt 23:15; γῆ, He 11:29.†

* **Associated Passages:** Genesis 1:9; Genesis 1:10; Hebrews 11:29; John 5:3; Jonah 1:9; Luke 6:6; Luke 6:8; Luke 23:31; Matthew 12:10; Matthew 23:15; Mark 3:3


## ξύλινος (id: ASL03730)

**ξύλινος**, \-η, \-ον  (ξύλον), \[in LXX chiefly for עֵץ;] 

*wooden*: II Ti 2:20, Re 9:20 (cf. Ep. Je 4 ff.).†

* **Associated Passages:** 2 Timothy 2:20; Letter of Jeremiah 1:4; Revelation 9:20


## ξύλον (id: ASL03731)

**ξύλον**, \-ου, τό,  \[in LXX chiefly for עֵץ;] 

1\. *wood*: I Co 3:12, Re 18:12.

2\. *a piece of wood*, hence, anything made of wood, as, 

(a) *a cudgel*, *staff*: pl., Mt 26:47, 55, Mk 14:43, 48, Lk 22:52;

(b) *stocks*, for confining the feet (Jb 33:11, סַד): Ac 16:24;

(c) a beam to which malefactors were bound (late Gk.), in LXX, of a gibbet (De 21:22, 23), in NT, of the Cross: Ac 5:30 10:39 13:29, Ga 3:13, I Pe 2:24.

3\. In late writers (v. MM, xvii), *a tree* (Ge 1:29, Is 14:8, al.): Lk 23:31; ξ. τῆς ζωῆς, Re 2:7 22:2, 14, 19.†

* **Associated Passages:** 1 Corinthians 3:12; 1 Peter 2:24; Acts 5:30; Acts 10:39; Acts 13:29; Acts 16:24; Deuteronomy 21:22; Deuteronomy 21:23; Galatians 3:13; Genesis 1:29; Isaiah 14:8; Job 33:11; Luke 22:52; Luke 23:31; Matthew 26:47; Matthew 26:55; Mark 14:43; Mark 14:48; Revelation 2:7; Revelation 18:12; Revelation 22:2; Revelation 22:14; Revelation 22:19


## ξυράω (id: ASL03732)

**ξυράω**, \-ῶ 

late form of ξυρέω (q.v.) : I Co 11:6 (ξυρᾶσθαι, Rec. and Edd., but v.s. ξύρω).†

* **Associated Passages:** 1 Corinthians 11:6


## ξυρέω (id: ASL03733)

**ξυρέω**, \-ῶ  (\< ξυρόν, *a razor*), \[in LXX (also \-άω) chiefly for גּלח pi., pu.;]  (no ex. of pres. \-έω),

*to shave*: pass. and mid., *to shave oneself*, *have oneself shaved*, aor., Ac 21:24; pf. (Att.), I Co 11:5.†

* **Associated Passages:** 1 Corinthians 11:5; Acts 21:24


## ξύρω (id: ASL03734)

**ξύρω**, rare form of ξυρέω (Veitch, s.v.), aor. mid., ξύρασθαιι (Bl., § 24, s.v.; Zorell, s.v., etc.) for \-ᾶσθαι (Rec., Edd.): I Co 11:6.†

* **Associated Passages:** 1 Corinthians 11:6
