## Ψ (id: ASL05838)

**Ψ, ψ**, ψῖ, τό, indecl., 

*psi, ps,*, the twenty\-third letter. As a numeral, ψʹ \= 700, ψ͵ \= 700,000\.



## ψάλλω (id: ASL05839)

**ψάλλω** \[in LXX chiefly for זמר pi. (Jg 5:3, Ps 7:17, al.), also for נגן pi. (I Ki 16:16ff., al.) ;]  

1\. *to pull, twitch, twang* (as a bowstring, etc.; Æsch., Eur., al.), hence,

2\. absol.,

(a) *to play* a stringed instrument with the fingers (Hdt., Plat., al.);

(b) later, *to sing to a harp, sing psalms* (LXX); in NT, *to sing a hymn, sing praise*: Ja 5:13; c. dat. pers., Ro 15:9 (LXX), Eph 5:19; dat. instr., I Co 14:15.†

* **Associated Passages:** 1 Corinthians 14:15; 1 Samuel 16:16; Ephesians 5:19; James 5:13; Judges 5:3; Psalms 7:17; Romans 15:9


## ψαλμός (id: ASL05840)

**ψαλμός**, \-οῦ, ὁ  (\< ψάλλω),  \[in LXX chiefly for מִזְמוֹר ;]  

1\. *a striking, twitching* with the fingers (Eur., al.), hence, *a striking* of musical strings (Æsch., al.), and hence in later writers,

2\. *a sacred song* sung to musical accompaniment, *a psalm* (LXX): I Co 14:26, Eph 5:19, Col 3:16; of OT psalms, Lk 24:44, Ac 13:33; βίβλος ψαλμῶν, Lk 20:42, Ac 1:20.†

*SYN.*: v.s. ὕμνος

* **Associated Passages:** 1 Corinthians 14:26; Acts 1:20; Acts 13:33; Colossians 3:16; Ephesians 5:19; Luke 20:42; Luke 24:44
* **Associated Articles:** ψάλλω (ID: `ASL05839@AbbottSmithGreekLexicon`); ὕμνος (ID: `ASL05468@AbbottSmithGreekLexicon`)

## ψυχή (id: ASL05863)

**ψυχή**, \-ῆς, ἡ, \[in LXX very freq. for נֶפֶשׁ, sometimes for לֵבַב ,לֵב, etc. ;] 

1\. *breath* (Lat. anima), *breath of life, life* (Hom., al.; in Arist., of *the vital principle*): Mt 6:25, Mk 3:4 10:45, Lk 12:22, Jo 10:11, Ac 20:10, 24, II Co 1:23, Phl 2:30, I Th 2:8, al.

2\. *the soul*, 

(a) as the seat of the will, desires and affections: Mt 26:38, Mk 12:30 (LXX) 14:34, Lk 1:46, Jo 10:24, Ac 14:2, Phl 1:27, al.; ἐκ ψυχῆς, *from the heart*, *heartily*: Eph 6:6, Col 3:23;

(b) as a periphrasis for person or self (freq. in translation from Semitic originals, v. M, *Pr.*, 87; Robinson, *Gospels*, 113 ff.; but also freq. in cl., v. LS, s.v. II, 2; Edwards, *Lex.*, App. A.): Mt 11:29, Mk 8:36, Ac 2:41, Ro 2:9, I Pe 3:20, al.; πᾶσα ψ., Ac 2:43 3:23 (LXX), Ro 13:1; ψ. ζῶσα (ζωῆς), I Co 15:45, Re 16:3;

(c) as the object of divine grace and eternal salvation: He 13:17, Ja 1:21 5:20, I Pe 1:9, 22 2:11 4:19, III Jo 2.

*SYN.*: v.s. νοῦς, πνεῦμα, ψυχικός, and cf. *ICC* on I Th 5:23, Lft., *Notes*, 88 f.

* **Associated Passages:** 1 Corinthians 15:45; 1 Peter 1:9; 1 Peter 1:22; 1 Peter 2:11; 1 Peter 3:20; 1 Peter 4:19; 1 Thessalonians 2:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:23; 2 Corinthians 1:23; 3 John 1:2; Acts 2:41; Acts 2:43; Acts 3:23; Acts 14:2; Acts 20:10; Acts 20:24; Colossians 3:23; Ephesians 6:6; Hebrews 13:17; James 1:21; James 5:20; John 10:11; John 10:24; Luke 1:46; Luke 12:22; Matthew 6:25; Matthew 11:29; Matthew 26:38; Mark 3:4; Mark 8:36; Mark 10:45; Mark 12:30; Mark 14:34; Philippians 1:27; Philippians 2:30; Revelation 16:3; Romans 2:9; Romans 13:1
* **Associated Articles:** νοῦς (ID: `ASL03707@AbbottSmithGreekLexicon`); πνεῦμα (ID: `ASL04351@AbbottSmithGreekLexicon`); ψυχικός (ID: `ASL05864@AbbottSmithGreekLexicon`)
