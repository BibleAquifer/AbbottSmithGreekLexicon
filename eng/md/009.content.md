## ἴδιος (id: ASL02556)

**ἴδιος**, \-α, \-ον (in Attic usually \-ος, \-ον),  \[in LXX chiefly for pers. suff., also for לָהֶם, etc.; (τὰ ἴ.) בֵּיתוֹ;]

(b) κατ’ ἰδίαν, *apart*, *privately*, *in private*: Mt 14:13, 23 20:17, Mk 4:34 7:33, Lk 10:23, Ac 23:19, al.

* **Associated Passages:** 1 Corinthians 11:21; 1 Corinthians 12:11; 1 Corinthians 15:23; 1 Corinthians 15:38; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 1 Timothy 5:8; 2 Timothy 1:9; 2 Timothy 4:3; Acts 1:25; Acts 2:6; Acts 4:23; Acts 20:28; Acts 23:19; Galatians 6:5; Hebrews 7:27; John 1:11; John 1:42; John 5:43; John 13:1; John 16:32; John 19:27; Jude 1:6; Luke 6:41; Luke 10:23; Luke 18:28; Matthew 14:13; Matthew 14:23; Matthew 20:17; Matthew 22:5; Mark 4:34; Mark 7:33; Wisdom of Solomon 10:1
